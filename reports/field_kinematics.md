## 2. What NGS `a` measures (clean tracks, speed > 1 m/s; Savitzky–Golay window 7 on x/y)

| estimator | r_with_ngs_a | median_ratio |
|---|---|---|
| total |a| from x/y | 0.874 | 1.205 |
| tangential |dv/dt| from x/y | 0.617 | 0.887 |
| normal (turning) from x/y | 0.567 | 0.687 |
| |d(reported speed)/dt| | 0.613 | 0.864 |

NGS `a` follows total acceleration, including turning, and is never negative, so it can't separate
propulsion from braking or cutting. Field and lab acceleration both use `a_s` = Savitzky–Golay
d(reported speed)/dt (window `games.accel_window_frames` = 5), the quantity the lab τ was fit to.

- Tracks: 251,107; with a glitch frame: 450 (0.18%).

  (15 by more than 0.5 m/s), and 6 would have a peak over 11 m/s
  (clean: 0).
