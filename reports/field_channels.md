# Field channels

Generated by `uv run python -m eeg.ngs.channels`. Clean tracks only (glitch tracks dropped), REG.
Peaks hold for 3 frames. Burst: first 1.5 s after the snap, plays starting below
1.0 m/s. Per-play, per-game and per-season tables are in `data/processed/field_*.parquet`
and the lab side in `data/processed/lab_channels.parquet` (not committed). Aggregates only.

- Plays: 250,657 (92% burst-eligible); player-games: 7,899;
  player-seasons: 686 (476 qualified, 287 prospects).
- Season statistic `top3` (`games.season_statistic`) over counting games (≥ 5 plays).
  Qualified: ≥ 100 plays and ≥ 6 counting games.
- Units: speed m/s, ke J/kg, acceleration m/s², power W/kg.
