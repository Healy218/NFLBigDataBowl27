# Tenure: does the explosion tail stay longer?

Generated by `uv run python -m eeg.survival.tenure`. Aggregates only. Secondary outcome (open decision 3).

- **At risk:** prospects who played and debuted before 2025: 260 (48 exits). Time = seasons from debut to the
  last season with a play; exit = no play in any later season through 2025; still playing in 2025 = censored.
- **Provisional exits:** a 2024 debut exits by missing 2025 alone; 6 prospects who missed a season and came back
  are not exits. Three classes give at most three seasons, so this is early tenure.
- Tails as in step 6 (top 20% of a measure within group × draft class, over the whole class). Cox models
